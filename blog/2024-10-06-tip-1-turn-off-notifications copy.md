Deep work is important.

Interruptions are aplenty.

My mental health... has room for improvement.

Trust me, it'll be okay. It's worth doing a "notification zero" type thing, and figure out what you really need to be notified about, instead of having this huge firehose of notifications, where 99% aren't urgent or important and don't deserve to interrupt you.

People can wait.

<!-- truncate -->

# On your laptop / machine

You don't need to be notified of every Slack message and e-mail and Workplace message and CI build failure and every new hackernews article and every new item in an RSS feed

# On your cellphone

Delete Slack, or at least turn off notifications

Mute WhatsApp groups
